# Privacy Policy for Wordkeep

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Wordkeep is a Markdown and text editor developed by Bernardo Ferrari. This policy covers the Android app `com.bernaferrari.remarkor`.

## Information on your device

Wordkeep does not require an account and does not include advertising or developer-operated analytics. The app does not send your notes, searches, or usage records to the developer or a collection server.

Notes and to-do lists are plain files. You can keep them in the app's private storage or choose shared device storage. Local settings and a database store information such as note colors, labels, pinned or starred status, and notebook paths.

## Files and permissions

Private notebook storage needs no shared-storage permission. If you choose shared storage, Wordkeep requests storage access so it can read, edit, search, and organize your notebook's Markdown and text documents and their folders. On Android 11 and later this uses the system's All files access setting; on older supported Android versions it uses storage permissions. You can revoke access in Android settings. Wordkeep does not upload the files it can access.

Other applications with access to a shared folder, including any sync tool you choose, may read or synchronize those files under their own policies. Wordkeep does not operate a cloud sync service.

## Connections and sharing

Wordkeep can open web links, handle files or text you send from another app, and share content through Android when you choose. Markdown may contain links to remote images or other external resources; opening or loading such content can contact the referenced service, which may receive information such as your IP address and the resource URL. External browsers, image hosts, recipients, and sync providers have their own privacy practices. Do not include private data in remote resource URLs unless you intend to send it to that service.

Wordkeep also exposes supported note actions through Android App Functions. Access by compatible system or assistant integrations is governed by Android's authorization controls. Content passed to an integration is subject to that integration's policies.

## Retention, deletion, and backups

You control your note files. Delete files you no longer need through Wordkeep or another file manager. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes private app data; files in shared storage may remain and must be deleted separately. Deleting a file can be permanent, so keep backups of important documents.

Android backup or device-transfer services may preserve eligible app data according to the operating system and your settings. Independently created backups and copies held by sync tools or recipients are managed by those services. Deleting data from Wordkeep does not necessarily delete those copies. The developer cannot access device backups and has no server-side note database to erase.

## Children

Wordkeep has no accounts, advertising, or developer-operated tracking and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
